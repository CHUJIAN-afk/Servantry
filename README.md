# Servantry 模组简介

## 中文版

---

### ⚔️ 泰拉瑞亚召唤师的终极梦想，在 Minecraft 中完美重现！

**Servantry** 是一款革命性的 Minecraft NeoForge 模组，以**至高还原度**将泰拉瑞亚的召唤师职业体验带入方块世界。告别传统实体限制，采用创新的**玩家附件存储架构**，实现流畅、高效、震撼的仆从战斗系统。

---

## 🌟 核心特性

### 🎯 智能仆从系统

仆从不再是简单的跟随者——它们是**拥有独立 AI 的战斗伙伴**：

- **智能目标搜索**：自动识别敌对生物、攻击玩家的敌人、以及伤害过主人的目标
- **优先级行为系统**：多层级 AI 目标选择器，支持瞬移、攻击、空闲等复杂行为
- **物理驱动运动**：基于力学的运动系统，支持施加力、冲量、阻力调节
- **路径追踪渲染**：平滑的贝塞尔曲线运动，配合华丽的拖尾特效

## 🔥 仆从详解

### 泰拉棱镜 (Terra Prism)

*传说中泰拉瑞亚最强召唤武器的完美复刻*

- **穿刺攻击**：穿过敌人造成伤害，可同时命中多个目标
- **目标追踪**：实时预测目标移动，动态调整攻击轨迹
- **32格索敌范围**：超远距离锁定敌人
- **华丽拖尾**：攻击时展现炫目的光棱拖尾效果

### 星尘细胞 (Stardust Cell)

*来自星尘的神秘力量，寄生、分裂、毁灭*

- **瞬移攻击**：瞬间传送至目标周围，发射星细胞射弹
- **玩家联动**：主人攻击时 33% 概率额外发射射弹
- **持续旋转**：恒定速度旋转动画，视觉震撼
- **24格索敌范围**：精准打击

### 刃杖 (Blade Staff)

*快速、精准、致命的飞刀风暴*

- **环绕飞行**：围绕主人旋转悬浮，形成护盾
- **快速穿刺**：高速穿过敌人，造成穿透伤害
- **多单位协同**：支持同时召唤多个飞刀，形成刀刃风暴
- **12格索敌范围**：近身防御利器

## 💫 射弹系统

### 星细胞射弹 (Stardust Projectile)

- **智能追踪**：发射后自动追踪目标

## 🌟 Core Features

### 🎯 Intelligent Servant System

Servants are not simple followers—they are **combat companions with independent AI**:

- **Smart Target Search**: Automatically identifies hostile mobs, enemies attacking the player, and entities that damaged the owner
- **Priority Behavior System**: Multi-layered AI goal selector supporting teleportation, attack, idle, and complex behaviors
- **Physics-Driven Movement**: Force-based motion system with applied forces, impulses, and drag adjustment
- **Path-Tracked Rendering**: Smooth Bézier curve movement with gorgeous trail effects

## 🔥 Servant Details

### Terra Prism

*A perfect recreation of Terraria's legendary strongest summon weapon*

- **Piercing Attack**: Passes through enemies dealing damage, can hit multiple targets
- **Target Tracking**: Real-time prediction of target movement, dynamic trajectory adjustment
- **32-Block Target Range**: Long-distance enemy lock-on
- **Gorgeous Trails**: Dazzling light prism trail effects during attacks

### Stardust Cell

*Mysterious power from the stars—parasitize, split, destroy*

- **Teleport Attack**: Instantly teleports near targets to fire stardust projectiles
- **Player Synergy**: 33% chance to fire extra projectile when owner attacks
- **Continuous Rotation**: Constant speed rotation animation, visually stunning
- **24-Block Target Range**: Precision strikes

### Blade Staff

*Fast, precise, deadly knife storm*

- **Orbital Flight**: Rotates and hovers around the owner, forming a shield
- **Rapid Piercing**: High-speed passes through enemies, dealing penetrating damage
- **Multi-Unit Coordination**: Supports summoning multiple blades simultaneously, creating a blade storm
- **12-Block Target Range**: Close-range defensive weapon
