# 📖 Servantry / 仆从学

---

## 中文版简介

### ⚔️ 仆从学 — 召唤你的私人军团

> **一人成军，万敌皆灭。让敌人在星尘与利刃中灰飞烟灭。**

---

**仆从学** 是一个为 Minecraft NeoForge 打造的战斗增强模组，引入了完整的仆从与射弹战斗系统。召唤自动战斗的仆从，释放追踪射弹，让敌人无处可逃。

---

### 💎 仆从一览

| 仆从 | 特性 |
|------|------|
| **星尘细胞** | 瞬移突袭、发射追踪射弹、玩家攻击联动射击 |
| **泰拉棱镜** | 碰撞攻击、穿敌而过、无敌帧穿透伤害 |
| **附魔飞刀** | 远程精准打击、连发压制 |

### 🚀 射弹系统

**星细胞射弹** — 追踪目标 → 黏贴寄生 → 叠加伤害 → 目标死亡爆裂

---

### 🎨 极致的视觉表现

这是仆从学最引以为傲的部分。

#### ✨ 动态拖尾系统

每一个仆从、每一枚射弹，都拖着一条 **流光溢彩的轨迹**：

- **锥形拖尾** — 如彗星划过夜空，渐变的光锥向后延伸
- **飘带拖尾** — 丝带般流动的轨迹，随风飘曳
- **颜色渐变** — 从核心到边缘，色彩自然过渡
- **淡出效果** — 轨迹末端优雅消散，如烟雾散去

#### 🌈 星尘细胞的视觉盛宴

当星尘细胞瞬移攻击时：

- 💫 **瞬移残影** — 原位置留下消散的光影
- ⚡ **飞行轨迹** — 穿越空间的流光拖尾
- 🔮 **恒定旋转** — 细胞本体持续旋转，折射光芒
- 🎯 **射弹追踪** — 发出的射弹拖着细长光带，追踪敌人

#### 🦠 星细胞射弹的寄生美学

射弹命中后：

- 📍 **黏贴效果** — 射弹嵌入目标体内，持续发光
- 📈 **叠加层次** — 多枚射弹黏贴，光效叠加增强
- 💥 **死亡爆裂** — 目标死亡瞬间，粒子四散飞溅

#### 🔷 泰拉棱镜的穿透之美

棱镜穿越敌人时：

- 🗡️ **光刃轨迹** — 锐利的几何光带
- ✖️ **穿透闪光** — 经过敌人的瞬间光芒爆发
- 🔄 **循环动画** — 待机时的优雅旋转

## English Version

### ⚔️ Servantry — Summon Your Personal Legion

> **One becomes an army, ten thousand enemies fall. Let your foes crumble into stardust.**

---

**Servantry** is a combat enhancement mod for Minecraft NeoForge, featuring a complete servant and projectile combat system. Summon autonomous battle servants, unleash tracking projectiles, and leave enemies nowhere to hide.

---

### 💎 Servant Roster

| Servant | Abilities |
|---------|-----------|
| **Stardust Cell** | Teleport assault, tracking projectiles, player attack synergy |
| **Terraprism** | Collision attack, pierce through enemies, invincibility penetration |
| **Enchanted Throwing Knives** | Precision ranged strikes, rapid-fire suppression |

### 🚀 Projectile System

**Stardust Projectile** — Track target → Attach & parasitize → Stack damage → Burst on death

---

### 🎨 Stunning Visual Presentation

This is Servantry's greatest pride.

#### ✨ Dynamic Trail System

Every servant, every projectile drags a **shimmering trail of light**:

- **Cone Trails** — Like comets across the night sky, gradient light cones extending backward
- **Ribbon Trails** — Flowing ribbon trajectories, drifting like silk
- **Color Gradients** — Natural color transitions from core to edge
- **Fade Effects** — Trails dissipate elegantly at the end, like smoke vanishing

#### 🌈 Stardust Cell's Visual Feast

When Stardust Cell teleports to attack:

- 💫 **Teleport Afterimage** — Fading light shadow left at original position
- ⚡ **Flight Trajectory** — Streaming light trail through space
- 🔮 **Constant Rotation** — Cell body continuously rotating, refracting light
- 🎯 **Projectile Tracking** — Fired projectiles drag slender light bands, chasing enemies

#### 🦠 Stardust Projectile's Parasitic Aesthetics

After projectile hits:

- 📍 **Attachment Effect** — Projectile embeds into target, continuously glowing
- 📈 **Stacked Layers** — Multiple attachments stack, light effects intensify
- 💥 **Death Burst** — Particles scatter spectacularly when target dies

#### 🔷 Terraprism's Piercing Beauty

When prism pierces through enemies:

- 🗡️ **Light Blade Trail** — Sharp geometric light bands
- ✖️ **Penetration Flash** — Light burst at the moment of passing through enemies
- 🔄 **Idle Animation** — Elegant rotation while at rest

---

### 🎮 Combat Experience

> *"When you swing your blade, Stardust Cells fire in tandem;*
> *When you summon, Terraprisms pierce through foes;*
> *Enemies see not one player — but a legion draped in starlight."*

- 🗡️ **Melee Synergy** — Player attacks trigger servant coordinated shots
- 🎯 **Auto-Targeting** — Servants automatically lock onto and pursue enemies
- 💀 **Chain Kills** — Projectile parasitism stacks for continuous harvesting
