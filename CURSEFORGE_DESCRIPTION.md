# Servantry

Servantry is a summoner-focused combat mod inspired by Terraria's summoner class. Summon servants that fight for you, and deploy sentries to hold the line.

## Gameplay

Summon weapons conjure AI-driven servants with distinct combat styles - melee brawlers, ranged strikers, orbiting guardians, and stationary turrets. Grow your army with accessories and armor sets, and command a mix of servants and sentries to suit every fight.
